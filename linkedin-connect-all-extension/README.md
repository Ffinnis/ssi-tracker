# LinkedIn Connect All Extension

This Chrome extension adds a "Connect All" button to LinkedIn search results pages, allowing you to automate the process of sending connection requests.

## Installation

1.  Open Chrome and navigate to `chrome://extensions/`.
2.  Enable the "Developer mode" toggle, usually located in the top-right corner.
3.  Click the "Load unpacked" button.
4.  Select the `linkedin-connect-all-extension` directory from your project files.

The extension should now be installed and active.

## Usage

1.  Navigate to a LinkedIn people search results page (e.g., `https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/people/...`).
2.  You will find a "Connect All" button added to the bottom center of the screen.
3.  Click the "Connect All" button to start sending connection requests to the people listed on the current page.

**Note:** Automating actions on LinkedIn may violate their terms of service and could potentially lead to restrictions on your account. Use this extension responsibly and at your own risk.
